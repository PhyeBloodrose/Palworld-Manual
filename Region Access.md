# Region Access
In this manual you will be able to restrict how much of Palworld you have access to depending on the 'player_access' option in the yaml. This page will try to explain how much of Palworld is unlocked per tier.

The choice is progressive, if you pick a later Tower Boss, any and all lower tiers will also be made available.

## From Start
- Windswept Island
- Marsh Island
- Eastern Wild Island
- Sea Breeze Archipelago

Technology up to Level 10 is available.

## Lily & Lyleen
- Isle of Silence
- Isle of Murmurs
- Bamboo Groves (Not including Twilight Dunes)
- Sunlit Isle
- Isle of Decayed Treasures
- Frostbitten Isle
- Crescent Moon Shore
- Deserted Islet
- Arena

Technology up to Level 20 is available.

## Axel & Orserk
- Mount Obsidian
- Phantom Isle
- Verdant Brook
- No.1 Wildlife Sanctuary
- Twilight Dunes (now included with Bamboo Groves)

Technology up to Level 30 is available.

## Marcus & Faleris
- Isle of Eternal Summer
- No.2 Wildlife Sanctuary
- Desiccated Dunes
- Scouring Islet
- Flamepulse Islet
- Oasis Isle

Technology up to Level 40 is available.

## Victor & Shadowbeak
- No.3 Wildlife Sanctuary
- Islet of Circular Ruins
- Isle of the Glacial Core
- Astral Mountains
- Foot of the World Tree

Technology up to Level 50 is available.

## Saya & Selyne
- Sakurajima
- Suncrest Islet

Technology up to Level 55 is available.

## Bjorn & Bastigor
- Feybreak

Technology up to Level 60 is available.

## Auri & Shaolong
- Sunreach
- Glacial Memento Islet
- Bicornis Islet
- Sandstone Gate Islet

Technology up to Level 70 is available.

## The World Tree
Just makes The World Tree and all of its related pals available. All technology is also available.
